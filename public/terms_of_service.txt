5. DAILY MODE AND LEADERBOARDS

Daily mode features a leaderboard that is public and visible to anyone who
visits the Site. If you play Daily mode and are signed in, or play via
Discord, your chosen or automatically generated username, along with your
wins, losses, and streaks, may be displayed publicly.

If you play Daily mode inside Discord without having set a username, we
will automatically generate one for you based on your Discord display name
so that you can appear on the leaderboard. You can change this at any time
from the username field in your account settings.

If you do not want your activity displayed on the public leaderboard, you
can play Daily mode on the web without signing in or setting a username, or
avoid linking a Discord account.
