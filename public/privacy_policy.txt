ANDROID APP

Vagudle is also available as an Android app. The app displays the same
Vagudle experience described in this policy and follows the same data
practices. The app-specific features and services are described below.

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  - Internet access, required to load the game and sync cloud save data
  - Notifications, used only if you turn on optional daily reminders in
    Settings; these reminders are scheduled entirely on your device
  - Receive boot completed, used to re-schedule your reminders after your
    device restarts
  - Loopback interface, used internally by the app's runtime to let its
    components communicate with each other on-device; no data leaves your
    device as a result

The app does not include any analytics or advertising software. It does
use Firebase Crashlytics, a crash-reporting service provided by Google, to
help us detect and fix crashes and errors. Crash reports include the error
message and stack trace, technical details collected automatically by
Crashlytics (such as your device model, operating system version, and an
installation identifier), and, if you are signed in with Google Play Games,
your account identifier, so we can investigate whether a crash is tied to a
specific account. Crashlytics does not receive your email address, cloud
save contents, or gameplay data.

The app also uses the following Google Play services:

  - Google Play Games Services, to offer Play Games sign-in, described in
    the CLOUD SAVE ACCOUNTS section below
  - Play Integrity, which lets our servers confirm that certain requests
    (such as submitting feedback or requesting a sign-in link) come from
    a genuine copy of the app. Google provides a signed verdict to us; we
    do not store it.
  - Play In-App Review, which may ask if you want to rate the app. Google
    handles any review you choose to submit.
  - Play Asset Delivery, which downloads the app's media files.

Home-screen widgets: the app offers optional Daily, Stats, and
Achievements widgets. They display data already stored on your device. The
Daily widget may also contact our servers in the background to refresh your
leaderboard rank, sending your sign-in token if you are signed in. No
additional data is stored as a result.

Backups: the app allows Android's automatic backup, so Google may back up
the app's local data (described under LOCAL STORAGE) to your Google account
according to your device's backup settings.

LOCAL STORAGE

Your game state, settings, and progress are stored locally in your browser
using localStorage. This data is not accessible to us unless you choose to
sign in for cloud save as described below. On Android, it may be included in
your device's automatic backup, as described above.

---

CLOUD SAVE ACCOUNTS

Signing in is optional. If you choose to sign in, you can sync your
achievements, statistics, settings, and selected background across devices.

Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google.
You can sign in with:

  - Google
  - GitHub
  - A sign-in link sent to your email address
  - Discord
  - Google Play Games (Android app only)

When you sign in, we store the following in a database to power cloud save:

  - Your account identifier, issued by Firebase (or, for Discord and Play
    Games sign-in, your Discord user ID or Play Games player ID,
    respectively)
  - Your email address and/or display name, depending on the sign-in method
    you use
  - Your achievement progress, word statistics, settings, selected
    background, and the dates you first played and last played

Your Discord username, Discord avatar image, Play Games display name, and
Play Games avatar image are not stored in our database. They are held only
inside the signed, encrypted session described below, and are re-fetched
from Discord or Google Play Games each time you sign in so we can display
them to you.

This data is retained until you request deletion. To request deletion of
your cloud save or account data, contact us at the email addresses above.

Your browser may also store a local sign-in session (using localStorage or
IndexedDB, not cookies) so you stay signed in between visits. If you sign
in with Discord or Play Games, this local session also includes an
encrypted refresh token issued by that provider, which lets the app keep
you signed in without asking you to log in again. This token is stored
only on your device, never on our servers, and cannot be used to access
your Discord or Google account beyond verifying your identity. If you sign
in with Google, GitHub, Discord, or Play Games, that sign-in is also
subject to the provider's own privacy policy. We never receive your
password from these providers.

If you sign in with an email link, your email address is passed to Resend,
the email delivery service we use to send the link. Resend processes it
only to deliver that message.

You may link a Discord account to an existing Google/GitHub/email account
that already has cloud save progress. Once linked, your Discord ID is
associated with that same account.

DUEL RESULTS

When you complete a duel, the following data is stored in a database to power
the leaderboard:

  - Your Discord ID
  - Whether you won or lost
  - The number of guesses used
  - Your guesses and the letter colors shown for them
  - The word used in the duel, its length, the dictionary, and the guess limit
  - The timestamps of when the duel was generated and completed

Completed duel results are retained until you delete your account or ask us
to delete them. Some in-progress duel data is deleted automatically after a
maximum of 4 weeks. When a duel finishes, an internal notification
containing only the duel ID is sent to a service we operate, which powers
Discord bot features. To request deletion of your data, contact us at the
email addresses above.

DAILY MODE

Daily mode is available on the web and inside Discord (as a Discord
Activity). What we store depends on how you play:

  On the web, without signing in:
    Your progress is stored locally in your browser only.

  Signed in, or playing via Discord:
    - A public daily leaderboard entry containing a username, wins, losses,
      current streak, and best streak, tied to your account
    - If you play Daily mode inside Discord and have not chosen a username,
      we automatically generate one from your Discord display name so you
      can appear on the leaderboard. You can change this at any time from
      the username field in your account settings.
    - Which Discord server and channel (or "standalone" if played outside a
      server) you played in, and the platform used
    - A snapshot of your guesses and letter colors, so you can resume a
      game you haven't finished
    - If you play as part of a Discord server or group, that group's
      combined win streak (not tied to your individual identity)

  Retention:
    - Leaderboard entries (username, wins, losses, streaks) and group streaks
      are retained indefinitely, similar to duel results
    - Some in-progress daily snapshots are deleted automatically after a
      maximum of 4 weeks
    - Other daily records, including your completed attempts, guesses, and
      which group/channel you played in for a given day, are retained until
      you delete your account or ask us to delete them

  Visibility:
    - The daily leaderboard is public and visible to anyone who visits the
      Site, showing username, wins, losses, and streaks
    - If you don't want to appear on the public leaderboard, you can play
      Daily mode on the web without signing in, or without setting a
      username, or without linking a Discord account

  Internal use of gameplay events:
    When you play Daily mode through Discord, gameplay events (such as your
    Discord ID, the group you're playing in, and your guesses) are sent in
    real time to an internal service we operate, which powers Discord bot
    features like activity notifications. This service is run by us and is
    not shared with any outside company.

ACCOUNT DELETION

If you are signed in, you can delete your account and its associated data
at any time from the Account section of Settings, which deletes this data
immediately. You can also request deletion at any time by contacting us at
the email addresses above. Either way, we delete:

  - Your cloud save data (achievements, stats, settings, background)
  - Your daily leaderboard entry (username, wins, losses, streaks)
  - Your daily attempts, including guess snapshots
  - Your duel results, including guesses

If you use the in-app deletion option, you can also choose to delete your
sign-in account with Firebase Authentication. If you contact us instead, we
will delete it on request.

To stop a deleted account from being silently restored by an old sign-in
session, we keep a minimal record containing your account identifier (and
any linked Discord or Play Games identifier) and the time of deletion. It
contains no gameplay data. Feedback you submitted is not linked to your
account and is deleted only on request.

Group-level data that isn't tied to a single identifiable person, such as a
Discord server's combined streak, is not deleted as part of an individual
account deletion request, since it reflects the group as a whole rather than
any one player.

FEEDBACK

When you submit feedback through the in-app feedback form, the following
data is stored:

  - The feedback message, category, and sentiment you provide
  - Your email address, if you choose to provide one
  - Your IP address and browser user agent
  - The timestamp of submission

This data is retained indefinitely and used only to review and respond to
feedback. To request deletion of your data, contact us at the email
addresses above.

---

SECURITY AND RATE LIMITING

To protect the Site from spam and abuse, our servers briefly check the IP
address of each request against automated rate limits (for example, to
prevent someone from flooding a feature with automated submissions).
Outside of the feedback form described above, these IP addresses are not
stored in a database or linked to your account; they are used only in the
moment to decide whether to allow or block a request.

On the web, the feedback form and email sign-in use Cloudflare Turnstile, a
bot-detection service. Turnstile runs a check in your browser and shares
technical signals, including your IP address, with Cloudflare to decide
whether you are a human. On the Android app, Play Integrity is used for the
same purpose instead, as described above.

SERVICE PROVIDERS

We rely on the following providers to run the Site and app. Each receives
the data needed to do its job and is subject to its own privacy policy:

  - Cloudflare: hosting, database, storage, bot detection, and traffic
    statistics

    services
  - GitHub and Discord: sign-in and Discord Activities, if you use them

Like any website, our hosting provider receives your IP address and browser
details when you load the Site.
