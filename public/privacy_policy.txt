ANDROID APP

Vagudle is also available as an Android app. The app displays the same
Vagudle experience described in this policy and follows the same data
practices; it does not collect any additional data of its own.

The app requests the following Android permission:

  - Internet access, required to load the game and sync cloud save data

The app does not include any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting
software. It does integrate with Google Play Games Services to offer
Play Games sign-in, described in the CLOUD SAVE ACCOUNTS section below.

---

LOCAL STORAGE

Your game state, settings, and progress are stored locally in your browser
using localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible
to us, unless you choose to sign in for cloud save as described below.

---

CLOUD SAVE ACCOUNTS

Signing in is optional. If you choose to sign in, you can sync your
achievements, statistics, settings, and selected background across devices.

Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google.
You can sign in with:

  - Google
  - GitHub
  - A sign-in link sent to your email address
  - Discord
  - Google Play Games (Android app only)

When you sign in, we store the following in a database to power cloud save:

  - Your account identifier, issued by Firebase (or, for Discord and Play
    Games sign-in, derived from your Discord user ID or Play Games player
    ID, respectively)
  - Your email address and/or display name, depending on the sign-in method
    you use
  - For Discord sign-in specifically: your Discord user ID, username, and
    avatar image URL
  - For Play Games sign-in specifically: your Play Games player ID, display
    name, and avatar image URL
  - Your achievement progress, word statistics, settings, and selected
    background

This data is retained until you request deletion. To request deletion of
your cloud save or account data, contact us at the email addresses above.

Your browser may also store a local sign-in session (using localStorage or
IndexedDB, not cookies) so you stay signed in between visits. If you sign
in with Google, GitHub, Discord, or Play Games, that sign-in is also
subject to the provider's own privacy policy. We never receive your
password from these providers.

You may link a Discord account to an existing Google/GitHub/email account
that already has cloud save progress. Once linked, your Discord ID is
associated with that same account.

---

DUEL RESULTS

When you complete a duel, the following data is stored in a database to power
the leaderboard:

  - Your Discord ID
  - Whether you won or lost
  - The number of guesses used
  - The word used in the duel
  - The timestamp of completion

This data is retained indefinitely. To request deletion of your data, contact
us at the email addresses above.

---

DAILY MODE

Daily mode is available on the web and inside Discord (as a Discord
Activity). What we store depends on how you play:

  On the web, without signing in:
    Your progress is stored locally in your browser only.

  Signed in, or playing via Discord:
    - A public daily leaderboard entry containing a username, wins, losses,
      current streak, and best streak, tied to your account
    - If you play Daily mode inside Discord and have not chosen a username,
      we automatically generate one from your Discord display name so you
      can appear on the leaderboard. You can change this at any time from
      the username field in your account settings.
    - Which Discord server and channel (or "standalone" if played outside a
      server) you played in, and the platform used
    - A snapshot of your in-progress guesses and letter colors, so you can
      resume a game you haven't finished
    - If you play as part of a Discord server or group, that group's
      combined win streak (not tied to your individual identity)

  Retention:
    - Leaderboard entries (username, wins, losses, streaks) and group streaks
      are retained indefinitely, similar to duel results
    - In-progress guess snapshots, and which group/channel you played in for
      a given day, are retained for a maximum of 4 weeks, after which they
      are deleted automatically

  Visibility:
    - The daily leaderboard is public and visible to anyone who visits the
      Site, showing username, wins, losses, and streaks
    - If you don't want to appear on the public leaderboard, you can play
      Daily mode on the web without signing in, or without setting a
      username, or without linking a Discord account

  Internal use of gameplay events:
    When you play Daily mode through Discord, gameplay events (such as your
    Discord ID, the group you're playing in, and your guesses) are sent in
    real time to an internal service we operate, which powers Discord bot
    features like activity notifications. This service is run by us and is
    not shared with any outside company.

---

ACCOUNT DELETION

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by
contacting us at the email addresses above. When you do, we delete:

  - Your cloud save data (achievements, stats, settings, background)
  - Your daily leaderboard entry (username, wins, losses, streaks)
  - Your in-progress daily attempts and guess snapshots
  - Your duel results and group daily results

Group-level data that isn't tied to a single identifiable person, such as a
Discord server's combined streak, is not deleted as part of an individual
account deletion request, since it reflects the group as a whole rather than
any one player.

FEEDBACK

When you submit feedback through the in-app feedback form, the following
data is stored:

  - The feedback message, category, and sentiment you provide
  - Your email address, if you choose to provide one
  - Your IP address and browser user agent
  - The timestamp of submission

This data is retained indefinitely and used only to review and respond to
feedback. To request deletion of your data, contact us at the email
addresses above.
